# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Awards and Recognition

## Overview

Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital has received numerous prestigious awards and recognition from national healthcare organizations, government bodies, and patient advocacy groups. These accolades reflect the hospital's commitment to excellence, patient safety, quality care delivery, and community service.

---

## National Accreditations

### NABH Accreditation

Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital holds NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) accreditation since 2010, India's most rigorous healthcare standard. This recognition validates compliance with international standards for patient safety, quality management, infection control, healthcare delivery processes, and emergency preparedness.

### NABL Certification

Laboratory services achieved NABL certification ensuring test accuracy and reliability. The certification covers biochemistry, hematology, immunology, and microbiology with quality control protocols and professional competency standards.

### ISO 9001:2015 Certification

Maintains ISO certification for quality management systems demonstrating commitment to consistent quality and customer satisfaction.
