# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Medicine and Pharmacy Services

## Overview

The Pharmacy and Medicine Services at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital provide comprehensive medication management for inpatients, outpatients, and emergency patients. Our NABH-accredited 24x7 in-house pharmacy ensures uninterrupted access to quality medicines. Located in Vijay Nagar, Sector 26, Indore, the pharmacy is integrated with the hospital's digital patient management system for seamless prescription processing, medication dispensing, and patient counseling.

## Medication Safety

### Prescription Verification

Every prescription undergoes verification by a licensed pharmacist before dispensing. Checks include dose appropriateness, drug interactions, allergy screening, duplicate therapy identification, and renal or hepatic dose adjustments.

### High-Alert Medications

High-risk medications including insulin, anticoagulants, chemotherapy agents, and opioids follow additional safety protocols with independent double-check, special labeling, and restricted access.

### Medication Error Prevention

Barcode scanning for patient identification, automated dispensing cabinets in critical areas, pharmacist intervention documentation, incident reporting systems, and regular safety audits ensure minimal medication errors.

## Digital Pharmacy Services

### E-Prescriptions

Doctors generate digital prescriptions linked to the patient's electronic health record. Prescriptions are transmitted directly to the pharmacy for dispensing, reducing wait times and transcription errors.

### Online Medicine Refill

Patients with chronic conditions can request medicine refills through the hospital's digital platform. Prescriptions are validated and medicines prepared for collection or delivery.

### Medication History Access

Complete medication history is maintained in the digital patient profile accessible to treating physicians and pharmacists, ensuring continuity and safety.

## Specialized Medication Services

### Chemotherapy Preparation

Dedicated cytotoxic preparation area with laminar airflow hoods for safe chemotherapy compounding. Trained oncology pharmacists prepare and verify all chemotherapy doses.

### TPN and IV Admixtures

Total parenteral nutrition and intravenous admixtures are prepared under aseptic conditions in the sterile compounding area.

### Anticoagulation Clinic

Managed anticoagulation services for patients on warfarin or direct oral anticoagulants with INR monitoring and dose adjustment.

## Medication Policies

### Generic Medicine Policy

Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital encourages generic medicine use where available and therapeutically equivalent, reducing patient costs without compromising quality.

### Controlled Substance Policy

Schedule H and H1 drugs are dispensed only against valid prescriptions with proper documentation and storage.

### Narcotic and Psychotropic Policy

Strict inventory control, double-lock storage, daily reconciliation, and audit trail compliance for all narcotic and psychotropic substances.

### Return and Disposal Policy

Unused or expired medicines are collected and disposed of as per regulatory guidelines. Patients are advised not to self-dispose medicines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What are the pharmacy timings at Lifeline Hospital?**
A: The in-house pharmacy operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for patient convenience.

**Q: Can I buy medicines without a prescription?**
A: Over-the-counter medicines are available. Prescription medications require a valid doctor's prescription.

**Q: Are generic medicines available?**
A: Yes, generic alternatives are available for most medications. Ask the pharmacist for options.

**Q: Can I get my medicines delivered to my room?**
A: Yes, inpatient medicines are delivered to the ward by the pharmacy team.

**Q: How do I refill my chronic disease medications?**
A: Visit the pharmacy with your prescription or use the online refill system through the hospital portal.

**Q: Does the pharmacy accept insurance for medicine purchases?**
A: Yes, cashless insurance is applicable for inpatient medication. Outpatient pharmacy billing follows insurance policy terms.

**Q: What should I do if I experience a medicine side effect?**
A: Contact the hospital immediately or visit the emergency department if the reaction is severe. Report all side effects to your treating physician.

**Q: Can I bring medicines from outside the hospital?**
A: Outside medicines are generally discouraged for inpatients to ensure quality and safety. Discuss with your treating physician.
