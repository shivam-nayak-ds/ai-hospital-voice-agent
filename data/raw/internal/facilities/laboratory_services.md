
# laboratory_services.md

## Overview

The laboratory at Lifeline Multi‑Speciality Hospital is a NABL‑accredited, state‑of‑the‑art clinical diagnostic center. It offers a comprehensive range of tests in biochemistry, hematology, microbiology, immunology, and pathology, supporting outpatient, inpatient, and emergency care.

## Available Services

- **Biochemistry** – liver function test, renal function test, lipid profile, blood sugar, electrolytes, cardiac markers (troponin, CK‑MB, BNP)
- **Hematology** – complete blood count, coagulation profile (PT/INR, aPTT), peripheral smear, bone marrow aspiration
- **Microbiology** – culture and sensitivity (blood, urine, sputum, wound swab), fungal culture, tuberculosis testing (GeneXpert, AFB smear)
- **Immunology** – thyroid profile, vitamin D, B12, tumor markers (CEA, CA‑125, PSA), autoimmune panel
- **Serology** – HIV, HBsAg, HCV, VDRL, dengue, typhoid, malaria
- **Histopathology and Cytology** – tissue biopsy, fine needle aspiration cytology (FNAC), Pap smear
- **Point‑of‑care testing** – blood gas, troponin, glucose, INR in emergency
- **Home sample collection** – for patients who cannot visit the hospital

## Features and Facilities

- Fully automated biochemistry and hematology analyzers
- Separate sections for sample collection, processing, and analysis
- Laminar flow hood for sterile work
- BSL‑2 facility for handling infectious samples
- Centralized laboratory information system (LIS) for seamless integration with patient records
- 24×7 sample acceptance for emergency tests
- Online report delivery to patients and doctors

## Operating Hours

- Main laboratory: 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM (sample collection); testing continues 24×7
- Emergency stat tests (e.g., troponin, CBC, blood gas): available 24 hours a day
- Home sample collection: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (advance booking required)

## Eligibility and Access Information

- Any patient with a valid prescription from a registered practitioner can avail laboratory services
- Walk‑in patients are welcome during operating hours
- Inpatients can have samples collected by nursing staff; reports are sent directly to the treating doctor via the LIS
- Telephonic/second opinions: reports can be shared via email or SMS on patient consent
