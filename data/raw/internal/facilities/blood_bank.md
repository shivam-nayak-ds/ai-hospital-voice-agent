# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Blood Bank Services

## Overview

The Blood Bank at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital is a licensed and fully equipped facility providing safe and timely blood and blood component services. Located within the hospital premises at Vijay Nagar, Sector 26, near C21 Mall in Indore, the blood bank operates as an integral part of the hospital's emergency and critical care infrastructure. The facility maintains adequate blood inventory to support surgical procedures, trauma care, obstetric emergencies, cancer treatments, and chronic transfusion needs. All blood products undergo rigorous testing and quality control to ensure maximum safety for recipients.

## Available Services

- Whole blood collection, processing, and storage
- Packed red blood cell preparation and distribution
- Fresh frozen plasma preparation and availability
- Platelet concentrate preparation and storage
- Cryoprecipitate preparation for clotting factor replacement
- Blood grouping and Rh typing
- Crossmatching and compatibility testing
- Antibody screening and identification
- Direct and indirect Coombs testing
- Blood donation camp organization
- Donor screening and counseling
- Autologous blood collection for elective surgeries
- Neonatal blood component preparation
- Massive transfusion protocol activation for emergencies

## Features and Facilities

The blood bank is equipped with modern blood collection chairs, automated blood component separators, and temperature-controlled storage units including blood bank refrigerators, platelet agitators, and deep freezers for plasma storage. All storage units have continuous temperature monitoring with alarm systems to maintain optimal conditions. The facility uses automated blood grouping analyzers and crossmatch systems to minimize human error. A dedicated quarantine area ensures unscreened blood is never released for use. Blood bags from certified manufacturers with integrated filters ensure safe collection. The laboratory maintains a computerized inventory management system for real-time blood stock tracking and expiry monitoring.

## Eligibility and Access Information

**Blood Donor Eligibility**:
- Age between 18 to 65 years
- Weight above 45 kilograms
- Hemoglobin above 12.5 g/dL
- No active infections or recent illness
- No recent tattoos or piercings within 6 months
- No history of high-risk behavior
- Not pregnant or breastfeeding
- Must not have donated blood within the past 3 months

**Blood Recipient Access**:
- Patients with valid hospital admission or emergency registration
- Replacement donation from family members when required
- Exchange blood transfusion for neonatal patients
- Cross-hospital blood issuance with proper documentation
- Insurance and government scheme patients have equal access

## Safety and Quality Standards

All donated blood units are tested for HIV, Hepatitis B surface antigen, Hepatitis C antibody, Syphilis, and Malaria using NABL-certified testing methods. Blood units testing positive for any infection are immediately discarded with proper biohazard disposal protocols. The blood bank follows NABH and national blood safety guidelines for all operations. Strict cold chain maintenance ensures blood product integrity from collection to transfusion. Every unit has a unique identification number traceable from donor to recipient. Crossmatching is performed before every transfusion to prevent adverse reactions. Regular external quality assessment and internal audits maintain testing accuracy.

## Patient Benefits

Patients at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital benefit from rapid blood availability during emergencies with minimal waiting time. The 24x7 availability ensures no delay in critical situations including trauma, surgery, and obstetric emergencies. Component separation technology allows one donation to help multiple patients. The hospital maintains a well-stocked inventory of all blood groups to minimize dependency on external sources. Replacement donation policies are transparent and clearly communicated. Voluntary blood donation drives are organized regularly to maintain adequate stock levels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How can I donate blood at Lifeline Hospital?**
A: Visit the blood bank between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM with a valid photo ID. You will be screened for eligibility, and the donation process takes approximately 30 minutes.

**Q: Is blood donation safe?**
A: Yes, blood donation is completely safe. Sterile disposable equipment is used for every donor, and trained phlebotomists perform the collection.

**Q: How long does it take to get blood during an emergency?**
A: Emergency blood issuance is available within 15 to 30 minutes after crossmatching for standard cases.

**Q: Can I store my own blood before surgery?**
A: Yes, autologous blood donation is available for elective surgeries. Discuss this option with your surgeon at least 2 weeks before surgery.

**Q: Which blood groups are available?**
A: All major blood groups including A, B, AB, and O in both positive and negative Rh types are maintained.

**Q: Is there a charge for blood?**
A: A processing fee is charged to cover testing, storage, and component preparation costs as per government regulations.
