# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Ambulance Services

## Overview

The Ambulance Services at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital provide 24-hour, 7-days-a-week emergency medical transportation for patients across Indore and surrounding areas. Operating from the hospital premises at Vijay Nagar, Sector 26, near C21 Mall in Indore, the ambulance fleet is equipped with advanced life support systems and staffed by trained paramedics to ensure critical care begins from the point of pickup. The service is an integral part of the hospital's comprehensive emergency care system, designed to provide seamless pre-hospital care and rapid transport to the emergency department.

## Available Services

- Basic Life Support ambulance for stable patient transport
- Advanced Life Support ambulance for critical patients
- Cardiac ambulance with defibrillator and cardiac monitoring
- Neonatal transport ambulance with incubator facility
- Inter-facility transfer for critically ill patients
- Emergency accident and trauma transport
- Hospital discharge transport for patients requiring medical supervision
- Event medical standby for large gatherings
- Airport and railway station medical pickup
- Scheduled non-emergency medical transport

## Features and Facilities

All ambulances are equipped with oxygen supply systems, suction equipment, stretchers with spinal immobilization capability, cervical collars and splints, automated external defibrillators, cardiac monitors, pulse oximeters, blood pressure monitoring equipment, emergency medication kits, intravenous fluid administration sets, and communication systems for real-time coordination with the emergency department. Advanced Life Support ambulances additionally carry ventilators, infusion pumps, advanced airway management equipment, and 12-lead ECG capability for pre-hospital diagnosis and transmission to the receiving hospital. Vehicles are GPS-tracked for optimal dispatch and route planning. Regular preventive maintenance ensures vehicle reliability.

## Operating Hours

- Emergency ambulance dispatch: 24 hours, 7 days a week, 365 days a year
- Scheduled transport booking: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Dispatch coordination: Round-the-clock availability
- Response time within Indore city: Typically 15 to 30 minutes
- Long-distance transfer coordination: Available 24x7 with advance booking preferred

## Eligibility and Access Information

- Emergency ambulance services are available to all individuals requiring urgent medical transport
- No hospital admission is required for emergency pickup
- Insurance cashless ambulance services available for eligible patients
- Hospital discharge transport requires advance booking through the nursing station
- Inter-facility transfer requires coordination between referring and receiving hospitals
- Non-emergency scheduled transport can be booked through hospital reception
- Patients of all ages including neonates, pediatric, adult, and geriatric populations are served

## Safety and Quality Standards

All paramedics are certified in Basic Life Support and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support protocols. Ambulances undergo daily equipment checks and weekly deep cleaning with infection control protocols. Medical equipment is calibrated and tested regularly to ensure functionality. Vehicles are sanitized between patient transports following strict infection prevention guidelines. The dispatch system follows triage-based prioritization to ensure the most critical patients receive the fastest response. All transport events are documented with patient condition reports transmitted to the receiving hospital. The service complies with NABH standards for emergency medical services.

## Patient Benefits

Patients benefit from rapid emergency response with trained paramedics providing pre-hospital stabilization. The direct coordination between ambulance team and emergency department ensures seamless handover with no treatment delays. Pre-hospital ECG transmission allows the cardiac catheterization lab to be activated before the patient arrives, saving critical time during heart attacks. GPS tracking enables family members to monitor ambulance location and estimated arrival time. Integration with the hospital's 24x7 emergency department ensures immediate specialist care upon arrival. Insurance coordination for eligible patients reduces financial burden during emergencies.

A: Contact the hospital reception for emergency ambulance dispatch. The service operates 24x7 throughout the year.

**Q: What is the ambulance response time?**
A: Within Indore city limits, ambulances typically arrive within 15 to 30 minutes depending on traffic and location.

**Q: Is the ambulance service free?**
