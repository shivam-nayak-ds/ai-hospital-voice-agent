# privacy_policy.md

## Purpose

This Privacy Policy outlines how Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital collects, uses, stores, and protects patients’ personal and medical information in compliance with applicable laws and NABH standards.

## Scope

This policy applies to all patients, their representatives, and any individual whose data is processed by the hospital, including electronic and physical records.

## Policy Details

- **Information Collected**: Name, contact details, age, gender, medical history, test results, insurance information, payment data, and any other information provided during registration, treatment, or billing.
- **Use of Information**: For diagnosis, treatment, billing, quality improvement, legal compliance, and communication regarding appointments and health reminders.
- **Data Sharing**: Information may be shared with treating doctors, nursing staff, insurance companies (with patient consent), government authorities when required by law, and third‑party service providers bound by confidentiality.
- **Data Storage**: Electronic health records are stored on secure servers with encryption. Physical records are kept in locked archives with limited access.
- **Retention**: Records are retained for at least 10 years as per statutory requirements; thereafter destroyed securely.
- **Patient Rights**: Access, correct, or request deletion of personal data (subject to legal exceptions). Complaints can be lodged with the hospital’s Grievance Officer.

## Patient Responsibilities

- Provide accurate and complete information.
- Inform the hospital of any changes to personal data.
- Keep their login credentials (for digital services) confidential.

## Hospital Responsibilities

- Implement technical and organisational measures to prevent unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.
- Train all staff on data privacy and confidentiality.
- Obtain explicit consent before sharing data for non‑treatment purposes.
- Notify patients promptly in case of a data breach affecting their information.

## Special Cases and Exceptions

- **Legal Requirements**: Data may be disclosed without consent when mandated by court order, public health authority, or police investigation.
- **Emergencies**: When a patient is unable to give consent, essential information may be shared with family or other treating hospitals.
- **Research**: Anonymised data may be used for research after ethics committee approval.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my health information be shared without my permission?**
A: Only for your treatment, payment, or as required by law. Otherwise, your written consent is needed.

**Q: How can I access my medical records?**
A: Submit a written request to the Medical Records Department. Digital access is available through our patient portal.

**Q: Can I request deletion of my records?**
A: We retain records as per legal mandates. After the retention period, data is securely destroyed.
