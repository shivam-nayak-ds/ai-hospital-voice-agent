# discharge_policy.md

## Purpose

The Discharge Policy outlines the process, responsibilities, and timelines for discharging patients from Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital, ensuring a safe transition from hospital to home or another care facility.

## Scope

This policy applies to all patients admitted for inpatient care, including medical, surgical, and obstetrics patients.

## Policy Details

- **Discharge Planning** begins within 24 hours of admission, involving the treating doctor, nursing staff, and social worker if needed.
- **Discharge Criteria**: Medical stability, ability to manage at home, availability of caregiver support, and no need for acute hospital care.
- **Discharge Time**: Standard discharge time is before 11:00 AM to allow bed availability for new admissions. Late discharges (after 11:00 AM) may incur an additional day’s charges.
- **Discharge Summary**: A detailed summary including diagnosis, treatment given, medications, follow‑up instructions, and lifestyle advice is provided in English/Hindi.
- **Medication Reconciliation**: The patient or caregiver is counselled on new medications, dosage, and side effects.
- **Follow‑up Appointment**: An appointment is scheduled before discharge; contact details for emergencies are shared.
- **Discharge Against Medical Advice (DAMA)**: Patients leaving against doctor’s advice must sign a LAMA (Leave Against Medical Advice) form after counselling about risks.

## Patient Responsibilities

- Inform the nursing staff of any concerns before discharge.
- Ensure a responsible adult is available to accompany and assist the patient.
- Follow the discharge instructions and take prescribed medications as advised.
- Attend follow‑up appointments.

## Hospital Responsibilities

- Prepare and hand over the discharge summary, bills, and prescriptions in a timely manner.
- Educate the patient/family about wound care, dietary restrictions, and signs of complications.
- Arrange for home healthcare or ambulance transport if required.
- Maintain a 24‑hour helpline for post‑discharge queries.

## Special Cases and Exceptions

- **Transfer to Another Facility**: Medical records, imaging, and referral notes will be provided.
- **Delayed Discharge**: If a bed is unavailable at a step‑down facility, the patient may stay until alternative arrangements are made.
- **Newborn Discharge**: Additional checks (immunization, hearing screening) are completed; car seat safety is advised.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I be discharged on a Sunday?**
A: Yes, discharge is possible any day, but some administrative services may be limited. Plan with the nursing team.

**Q: What if I need home nursing?**
A: Our discharge coordinator can provide a list of empanelled home healthcare providers.

**Q: Will my insurance claim be processed before discharge?**
A: Cashless claims are settled before discharge. Reimbursement claims require you to submit documents to your insurer.
