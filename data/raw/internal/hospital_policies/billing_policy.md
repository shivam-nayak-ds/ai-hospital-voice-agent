# billing_policy.md

## Purpose

The Billing Policy establishes transparent, fair, and consistent billing practices at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital to ensure patients understand charges, payment methods, and financial responsibilities.

## Scope

This policy applies to all services rendered to inpatients, outpatients, emergency cases, and day‑care patients.

## Policy Details

- **Estimation**: A cost estimate is provided before elective procedures or admissions. It includes room charges, doctor fees, diagnostic tests, medicines, and consumables.
- **Room Rent**: Charged per day on a 24‑hour cycle. Check‑in before 12:00 PM counts as a full day; after 12:00 PM, the first day is counted from check‑in to next day 12:00 PM.
- **Consumables and Implants**: Charged at list price. Patients may be informed of high‑cost items in advance.
- **Billing Cycle**: Interim bills are issued every 3–5 days for long stays; final bill at discharge.
- **Payment Methods**: Cash, debit/credit cards, UPI, net banking, and cheques accepted.
- **Insurance**: Cashless facility for tie‑up insurance companies (list available at reception). For non‑network insurance, patients pay upfront and claim reimbursement.
- **Discounts**: No discount on room rent or doctor fees except for approved corporate or charitable cases.

## Patient Responsibilities

- Verify insurance coverage and pre‑authorisation before admission.
- Provide correct personal and insurance details at registration.
- Clear all dues at the time of discharge. Delay may result in additional charges.
- Keep all payment receipts for insurance claims and tax purposes.

## Hospital Responsibilities

- Provide itemised bills detailing each charge.
- Explain any billing queries within 30 minutes during business hours.
- Offer a dedicated billing helpdesk for insurance‑related issues.
- Refund any overpayment within 7 working days.

## Special Cases and Exceptions

- **Emergency Admissions**: Billing is initiated after stabilisation. Insurance pre‑authorisation may be applied retroactively.
- **Foreign Patients**: Payment in USD or equivalent is accepted; a discharge letter will be provided.
- **Charity Cases**: Eligible low‑income patients may receive partial or full waiver after social worker assessment and approval by the Medical Superintendent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I get an advance estimate for my surgery?**
A: Yes, the billing department provides a written estimate. Actual cost may vary slightly based on complications.

**Q: Why are there charges for consumables like gloves and syringes?**
A: These are itemised for transparency. They are included in the overall cost of care.

**Q: How do I claim reimbursement from my insurance?**
A: Collect a detailed final bill, discharge summary, and all investigation reports. Submit to your insurance company along with the claim form.
