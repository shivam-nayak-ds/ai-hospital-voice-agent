# visitor_policy.md

## Purpose

The purpose of this Visitor Policy is to ensure a safe, secure, and healing environment for all patients, visitors, and staff at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital. It establishes guidelines for visitor access, conduct, and duration to minimize disruption to patient care and infection risks.

## Scope

This policy applies to all visitors entering Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital premises, including family members, friends, legal representatives, and other guests. It covers general wards, semi‑private rooms, private rooms, ICU, NICU, and other specialised units.

## Policy Details

- **Visiting Hours**: General wards – 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM daily. ICU/CCU – 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM; only one visitor at a time.
- **Number of Visitors**: Maximum two visitors per patient at any time. Additional visitors may be allowed based on patient condition and unit policy.
- **Identification**: All visitors must register at the reception desk, provide valid ID proof, and wear a visitor badge.
- **Health Screening**: Visitors with fever, cough, cold, or other contagious symptoms are not permitted entry.
- **Children**: Children under 12 years are not allowed in ICU, NICU, and operation theatre areas unless special permission is granted.
- **Restricted Areas**: Operation theatres, sterile zones, and labour rooms are strictly restricted to authorised personnel.
- **Conduct**: Visitors must maintain silence, avoid mobile phone usage in patient care areas, and follow infection control measures (hand hygiene, mask wearing when required).

## Patient Responsibilities

- Inform visitors about hospital timings and rules.
- Limit the number of visitors to avoid crowding.
- Follow staff instructions regarding visitor entry and exit.

## Hospital Responsibilities

- Provide clear signage and information about visiting hours and rules.
- Maintain a visitor register and issue badges.
- Ensure security personnel enforce the policy.
- Allow exceptions for compassionate reasons (e.g., terminally ill patients) after approval from the treating doctor and nursing supervisor.

## Special Cases and Exceptions

- **Critical / Terminal Patients**: Immediate family members may be allowed extended hours with permission from the Medical Superintendent.
- **Emergency Admissions**: Visitors may wait in designated waiting areas until the patient is stabilised.
- **Religious or Cultural Needs**: Hospital may accommodate reasonable requests (e.g., prayer rituals) without compromising patient care.
- **COVID‑19 / Infectious Diseases**: Restricted visiting as per infection control protocol; virtual visits encouraged.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I stay overnight with my patient?**
A: Overnight stays are permitted only in private rooms with a fold‑out bed, subject to availability and nurse approval.

**Q: What if I miss visiting hours?**
A: Contact the nursing station. Exceptions may be made for genuine emergency circumstances.

**Q: Are mobile phones allowed inside ICU?**
A: No, mobile phones interfere with medical equipment. Please use the waiting area.
