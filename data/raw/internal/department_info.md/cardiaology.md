# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Cardiology Department

## Department Overview

The Cardiology Department at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital provides comprehensive cardiac care in Indore with 500 beds, 50 ICU beds, and advanced diagnostic facilities. Our NABH-accredited department operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM with 24x7 emergency services and features a state-of-the-art Advanced Cath Lab.

---

## Conditions Treated

The department manages coronary artery disease, acute coronary syndrome, heart failure, valvular disease, arrhythmias, hypertension, peripheral arterial disease, aortic disease, congenital heart disease, and myocardial conditions. Each condition receives specialized evaluation and treatment planning tailored to individual patient needs.

---

## Common Symptoms

Seek cardiology consultation for chest pain or discomfort, shortness of breath, palpitations, syncope, edema, and exercise intolerance. Chest pain described as pressure or heaviness radiating to the arm, neck, or back may indicate cardiac involvement. Persistent dyspnea, especially with exertion or at night, warrants evaluation.

---

## When to Consult a Cardiologist

Consult for persistent chest pain, unexplained dyspnea, recurrent palpitations, syncope, abnormal ECG findings, or multiple cardiovascular risk factors. Preventive consultation is recommended for family history of cardiac disease, diabetes, smoking, hypertension, or high cholesterol. Post-event consultations ensure proper recovery and optimization.

---

## Diagnostic Services

**Available Diagnostics**:
- 12-lead ECG and continuous monitoring
- Transthoracic and transesophageal echocardiography
- 24-hour Holter monitoring and event recording
- Exercise stress testing and pharmacological stress echocardiography
- Cardiac CT angiography with calcium scoring
- Cardiac MRI for tissue characterization
- Blood pressure monitoring (office, home, ambulatory)
- Cardiac biomarkers (troponin, BNP)
- Coronary angiography with intravascular imaging

All diagnostic services are interpreted by experienced cardiologists with NABL-certified laboratory support.

---

## Procedures and Treatments

**Interventional Procedures**:
- Percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) with stent placement
- Coronary atherectomy for calcified lesions
- Balloon valvuloplasty
- Pacemaker implantation
- Implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD) placement
- Cardiac resynchronization therapy (CRT)
- Radiofrequency ablation for arrhythmias
- Intravascular imaging (IVUS, OCT)

---

## Advanced Cath Lab Services

The Advanced Catheterization Laboratory provides diagnostic coronary angiography, emergency primary PCI for acute MI with door-to-balloon time <90 minutes, right heart catheterization for hemodynamic assessment, and pediatric interventional procedures. Equipped with high-definition fluoroscopy, 3D mapping systems, and real-time imaging guidance, the lab ensures optimal outcomes for all procedures.

---

## Emergency Cardiac Care

**24x7 Emergency Protocols**:
- Acute coronary syndrome: Rapid ECG, troponin testing, STEMI identification
- Primary PCI for STEMI with immediate catheterization lab activation
- Cardiogenic shock management with inotropic and mechanical support
- Acute arrhythmia management including defibrillation
- Aortic dissection emergency management
- Pulmonary embolism risk stratification and treatment

Emergency medications include aspirin, P2Y12 inhibitors, heparin, nitrates, and beta-blockers administered per protocols.

---

## Preventive Cardiology

**Risk Factor Management**:
- Hypertension control (target <130/80 mmHg)
- Lipid management with statins and other agents
- Diabetes management with cardioprotective medications
- Smoking cessation support
- Lifestyle counseling (diet, exercise, stress management, weight loss)
- Cardiac rehabilitation programs (Phase 1-3)
- Screening and prevention initiatives

## Inpatient and Outpatient Services

**Inpatient Services**: Acute coronary syndrome care, heart failure hospitalization, post-intervention monitoring, telemetry monitoring, and intensive cardiac care.

**Outpatient Services**: Cardiology clinic consultations, follow-up care, device clinic interrogations, cardiac rehabilitation center, advanced heart failure clinic, and telemedicine consultations with remote monitoring capabilities.

## Patient Journey

Initial consultation includes comprehensive history, physical examination, ECG, and diagnostic test ordering. The diagnostic phase involves testing, result interpretation, and additional imaging as needed. Treatment planning encompasses medication initiation, lifestyle modification, and intervention timing. Post-intervention follow-up ensures proper recovery with office visits at 1-2 weeks and regular surveillance thereafter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What does cardiac chest pain feel like?**
A: Typically pressure or heaviness in the center of the chest, possibly radiating to the arm, neck, or back with associated shortness of breath or sweating.

**Q: When should I go to the emergency department?**
A: Seek immediate care for severe chest pain, persistent dyspnea, syncope, or severe palpitations with chest discomfort.

**Q: Do I need a stent?**
A: Stents are recommended for significant stenosis causing ischemia. Medical management may be appropriate for other lesions.

**Q: Can I exercise after a heart attack?**
A: Yes, exercise is beneficial through supervised cardiac rehabilitation with gradual progression.

**Q: Can I stop my cardiac medications if I feel better?**
A: Never stop medications without consulting your cardiologist, even if symptoms improve.
