# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Dermatology Department

## Dermatology Department Overview

The Dermatology Department at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital provides comprehensive skin, hair, and nail care in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Located in Vijay Nagar, Sector 26, near C21 Mall, the NABH-accredited department operates within a 500-bed facility with NABL-certified laboratory support, advanced diagnostic facilities, and modern treatment technology. Services are available from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM with emergency coverage for severe dermatological conditions.

---

## Conditions Treated

The department manages acne and rosacea, eczema and dermatitis, psoriasis, skin infections (bacterial, fungal, viral), vitiligo and pigmentation disorders, urticaria and angioedema, alopecia and hair loss, nail disorders, skin cancer and precancerous lesions, autoimmune skin diseases (lupus, pemphigus), allergic contact dermatitis, keloids and hypertrophic scars, fungal infections, herpes and viral warts, and chronic skin ulcers.

---

## Common Symptoms

Consult a dermatologist for persistent rashes or redness, unexplained itching, new or changing moles, chronic acne unresponsive to over-the-counter treatment, hair thinning or sudden hair loss, nail discoloration or deformity, skin darkening or pigmentation changes, recurrent skin infections, non-healing wounds or ulcers, blistering or peeling skin, and allergic skin reactions with swelling or hives.

---

## When to Consult a Dermatologist

Seek consultation for skin conditions persisting beyond two weeks despite basic care, rapidly changing moles or lesions, severe acne causing scarring, chronic eczema or psoriasis management, hair loss exceeding normal shedding, suspected skin infections, cosmetic concerns affecting quality of life, and autoimmune skin disease evaluation. Emergency dermatology care is needed for severe drug reactions with skin involvement, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, and widespread blistering disorders.

---

## Diagnostic Services

**Skin Biopsy and Histopathology**: Punch, shave, and excisional biopsy for definitive diagnosis of suspicious lesions, inflammatory conditions, and autoimmune diseases.

**Patch Testing**: Identification of contact allergens causing allergic dermatitis through standardized allergen panels.

**Dermoscopy**: Non-invasive magnified skin examination for early melanoma detection and mole evaluation.

**Wood's Lamp Examination**: Ultraviolet light assessment for fungal infections, pigmentation disorders, and bacterial infections.

**Skin Scraping and Culture**: Fungal and bacterial identification for targeted antimicrobial therapy.

**Blood Tests**: NABL-certified laboratory testing for autoimmune markers, allergy panels, nutritional deficiencies, and hormonal assessment related to skin conditions.

---

## Skin Allergy Management

Comprehensive allergy evaluation includes detailed history, patch testing for contact allergens, and prick testing for environmental triggers. Management involves allergen avoidance counseling, topical corticosteroids for acute flares, antihistamine therapy, immunomodulatory agents for chronic conditions, and desensitization protocols when appropriate.

---

## Acne and Pigmentation Care

Acne management follows severity-based grading with topical retinoids, antibiotics, hormonal therapy, and isotretinoin for severe cases. Scar treatment includes chemical peels, microneedling, and laser therapy. Pigmentation disorders including melasma, post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, and vitiligo receive personalized treatment plans combining topical agents, phototherapy, and procedural interventions.

---

## Hair and Scalp Disorders

Hair loss evaluation includes scalp examination, trichoscopy, blood work, and scalp biopsy when indicated. Androgenetic alopecia is managed with minoxidil, finasteride, and PRP therapy. Alopecia areata receives intralesional corticosteroids and immunotherapy. Scalp infections and inflammatory conditions are treated with targeted antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory agents.

---

## Nail Disorders

Evaluation of nail changes including discoloration, thickening, brittleness, and deformity. Fungal nail infections receive oral and topical antifungal therapy. Nail psoriasis and lichen planus are managed with targeted treatments. Nail biopsy is performed for suspicious lesions. Ingrown nail management includes conservative and surgical approaches.

---

## Cosmetic Dermatology Services

**Available Services**: Chemical peels for skin rejuvenation, microneedling for scar revision and collagen induction, laser therapy for pigmentation and vascular lesions, botulinum toxin for wrinkles, dermal fillers for volume restoration, PRP therapy for skin and hair rejuvenation, and wart and skin tag removal.

---

## Treatments and Procedures

**Medical Treatments**: Topical corticosteroids and immunomodulators, systemic immunosuppressants for autoimmune conditions, biologic therapy for severe psoriasis, antifungal and antiviral therapy, phototherapy (narrowband UVB) for psoriasis and vitiligo, and intralesional injections for keloids and alopecia.

**Surgical Procedures**: Excision of skin tumors and cysts, cryotherapy for warts and precancerous lesions, electrocautery for benign growths, and Mohs surgery coordination for skin cancer.

---

## Inpatient and Outpatient Services

**Inpatient**: Severe drug reactions, erythroderma management, autoimmune blistering diseases, and cellulitis requiring IV antibiotics. **Outpatient**: General dermatology consultations, cosmetic procedures, phototherapy sessions, patch testing, and telemedicine follow-ups for chronic conditions.

---

## Preventive Skin Health

- Regular sunscreen use (SPF 30+ broad spectrum)
- Monthly self-examination for changing moles
- Annual skin cancer screening for high-risk individuals
- Gentle skincare routine with appropriate moisturizers
- Avoidance of excessive sun exposure during peak hours
- Early treatment of skin infections to prevent spread
- Balanced nutrition supporting skin health
- Stress management for conditions like psoriasis and eczema

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When should I worry about a mole?**
A: Consult if a mole changes in size, color, shape, or becomes asymmetric with irregular borders. Use the ABCDE criteria for assessment.

**Q: Can acne scars be treated?**
A: Yes, acne scars improve significantly with chemical peels, microneedling, laser therapy, and dermal fillers depending on scar type.

**Q: Is hair loss reversible?**
A: Some types are reversible including telogen effluvium and alopecia areata. Androgenetic alopecia is manageable but progressive.

**Q: How is psoriasis managed?**
A: Treatment includes topical agents, phototherapy, systemic medications, and biologic therapy based on severity and extent.

**Q: Are cosmetic dermatology procedures safe?**
A: Performed by qualified dermatologists, cosmetic procedures have excellent safety profiles with minimal downtime.

**Q: Can vitiligo be cured?**
A: Vitiligo is manageable with phototherapy, topical treatments, and surgical options. Complete cure varies by individual.
