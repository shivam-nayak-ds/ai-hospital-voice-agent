# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Gastroenterology Department

## Gastroenterology Department Overview

The Gastroenterology Department at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital provides comprehensive digestive care in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Located in Vijay Nagar, Sector 26, near C21 Mall, the NABH-accredited department operates within a 500-bed facility with 50 ICU beds, advanced endoscopy suites, and NABL-certified laboratory support. Services are available from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM with 24x7 emergency coverage.

---

## Conditions Treated

The department manages gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), peptic ulcers, inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's and ulcerative colitis), irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), liver cirrhosis and hepatitis, pancreatitis, gallstone disease, colorectal polyps and cancer, celiac disease, gastrointestinal bleeding, esophageal disorders, and functional digestive disorders.

---

## Common Symptoms

Consult for persistent heartburn or acid reflux, chronic abdominal pain, unexplained weight loss, difficulty swallowing, blood in stool or black tarry stools, chronic diarrhea or constipation, jaundice, nausea and vomiting, bloating and gas, and loss of appetite. Acute severe abdominal pain with fever requires emergency evaluation.

---

## When to Consult a Gastroenterologist

Seek consultation for persistent digestive symptoms lasting more than two weeks, family history of colorectal cancer or polyps, age-appropriate screening colonoscopy, chronic liver disease management, unexplained anemia, and recurrent digestive complaints despite treatment. Urgent referral is needed for gastrointestinal bleeding, severe jaundice, and acute pancreatitis symptoms.

## Diagnostic Services

- **Upper GI Endoscopy (OGD)**: Visualization of esophagus, stomach, and duodenum
- **Colonoscopy**: Complete colon evaluation and polyp removal
- **ERCP**: Biliary and pancreatic duct assessment and intervention
- **Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS)**: Tissue sampling and staging
- **Liver FibroScan**: Non-invasive liver stiffness measurement
- **CT Scan and MRI Abdomen**: Cross-sectional imaging for detailed assessment
- **Capsule Endoscopy**: Small bowel visualization
- **NABL-Certified Lab**: Liver function tests, pancreatic enzymes, stool analysis, hepatitis markers

---

## Endoscopy and Colonoscopy Services

Advanced endoscopy services include diagnostic and therapeutic upper GI endoscopy, screening and surveillance colonoscopy with polypectomy, ERCP for bile duct stones and strictures, endoscopic mucosal resection for early tumors, stent placement for obstruction, and foreign body removal. All procedures are performed under sedation with continuous monitoring by trained anesthesiology staff.

---

## Liver and Pancreatic Disorders

Comprehensive management covers hepatitis B and C with antiviral therapy, liver cirrhosis monitoring and complication management, fatty liver disease with lifestyle intervention, autoimmune hepatitis, acute and chronic pancreatitis, pancreatic cyst evaluation, gallstone-related disorders, and hepatobiliary cancer evaluation with multidisciplinary tumor board coordination.

## Digestive Health Management

Personalized treatment plans address GERD with proton pump inhibitor therapy, IBS with dietary modification and targeted medications, inflammatory bowel disease with biologic therapy, celiac disease with gluten-free diet counseling, and functional dyspepsia with comprehensive approach. Nutrition counseling and dietitian support are integrated into care.

---

## Treatments and Procedures

Therapeutic endoscopy includes variceal band ligation, hemostasis for active bleeding, balloon dilation for strictures, percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) tube placement, polypectomy, and biliary intervention via ERCP. Medical management follows evidence-based guidelines with regular monitoring and follow-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is endoscopy painful?**
A: Endoscopy is performed under sedation ensuring patient comfort. Most patients report minimal discomfort.

**Q: How often should I get a colonoscopy?**
A: Routine screening begins at age 45. Follow-up intervals depend on findings and risk factors.

**Q: Can GERD be cured?**
A: GERD is manageable with medication and lifestyle modification. Long-term treatment may be needed.

**Q: What are signs of liver disease?**
A: Jaundice, abdominal swelling, fatigue, dark urine, and unexplained weight loss indicate liver problems.

**Q: Is colonoscopy preparation difficult?**
A: Bowel preparation requires dietary restriction and laxative intake. Modern preparations are better tolerated.

**Q: Can fatty liver disease be reversed?**
A: Yes, early fatty liver disease is reversible with weight loss, exercise, and dietary changes.
