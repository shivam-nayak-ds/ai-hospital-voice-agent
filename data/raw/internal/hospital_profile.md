# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Hospital Profile

## Hospital Overview

Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital is a premier private healthcare facility located in Vijay Nagar, Sector 26, near C21 Mall in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. Established in 2005, the hospital has evolved into a comprehensive multi-speciality institution providing advanced medical and surgical care to patients across the region. With a commitment to quality, safety, and patient-centered care, the hospital serves as a trusted healthcare destination for acute care, chronic disease management, and preventive medicine.

### Hospital Statistics

- **Bed Capacity**: 500 total beds
- **ICU Beds**: 50 dedicated intensive care beds
- **Specialists**: 120+ experienced doctors across 10+ specialties
- **Staff**: 500+ healthcare professionals including nurses and technicians
- **Annual Consultations**: 50,000+ outpatient visits
- **Emergency Cases**: 15,000+ emergency admissions annually

## Accreditations and Quality Standards

### NABH Accreditation

Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital holds prestigious NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) accreditation, signifying compliance with international standards. The accreditation validates standards for patient safety, quality management, infection control, and healthcare delivery. The hospital undergoes regular audits ensuring continuous compliance and improvement.

**NABH Standards Include**:
- Patient safety protocols and risk management
- Quality management systems
- Infection prevention and control measures
- Staff training and competency programs
- Medical records management
- Emergency preparedness

### NABL Laboratory Certification

The laboratory services hold NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) certification, ensuring accurate and reliable test results. NABL certification covers biochemistry, hematology, immunology, and microbiology testing with international standards compliance.

### ISO 9001:2015 Certification

The hospital maintains ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management systems, demonstrating commitment to consistent quality and customer satisfaction.
