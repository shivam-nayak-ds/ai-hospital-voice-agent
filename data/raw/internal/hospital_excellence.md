# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Centers of Excellence

## Overview

Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital has developed specialized centers of excellence in key medical fields, combining concentrated expertise, advanced technology, and comprehensive services. These centers represent the hospital's commitment to delivering world-class care while fostering innovation and continuous clinical improvement.

---

## Advanced Cardiac Care Center

### Services and Capabilities

The Advanced Cardiac Care Center provides comprehensive cardiovascular care from prevention through complex cardiac condition treatment. The state-of-the-art Advanced Cath Lab features digital fluoroscopy, real-time imaging guidance, and intravascular ultrasound capabilities.

**Diagnostic Services**:
- Coronary angiography and functional assessment
- Transthoracic and transesophageal echocardiography
- Stress testing and exercise protocols
- Cardiac CT and MRI imaging
- Holter monitoring and event recording

**Interventional Procedures**:
- Percutaneous coronary intervention with stent placement
- Pacemaker and ICD implantation
- Radiofrequency ablation for arrhythmias
- Balloon valvuloplasty

**Acute Cardiac Care**:
- 24x7 emergency response with door-to-balloon time <90 minutes for STEMI
- Cardiogenic shock management
- Acute heart failure treatment

### Outcomes

- 95%+ procedural success rate for PCI
- In-hospital major adverse cardiac event rate <2%
- 30-day mortality post-MI <5% in eligible patients

## Trauma and Orthopedic Center

### Comprehensive Trauma Care

The center operates an integrated trauma management system with rapid assessment, emergency surgical capability, and structured rehabilitation. The dedicated trauma unit handles polytrauma patients with damage control orthopedics protocols and multidisciplinary team coordination.

**Services Offered**:
- 24x7 trauma resuscitation
- Fracture fixation (open and closed techniques)
- Joint replacement (hip, knee, shoulder)
- Arthroscopic procedures
- Sports injury management
- Comprehensive rehabilitation programs

### Quality Metrics

- 95% fracture healing rate with minimal complications
- 90% patient satisfaction with joint replacement outcomes
- Rapid return to function through early mobilization protocols
- Low infection and complication rates through evidence-based care

## Neurology and Stroke Center

### Acute Stroke Management

The center follows evidence-based protocols for both ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke. The 24x7 stroke unit provides rapid diagnosis and treatment, with thrombolytic therapy available within therapeutic window.

**Key Services**:
- 24-hour EEG monitoring with seizure detection
- Electrodiagnostic testing (EMG/NCS)
- MRI and advanced neuroimaging
- Neurointensive care monitoring
- Comprehensive neurorehabilitation

**Specialist Services**:
- General neurology consultations
- Neurosurgery coordination
- Neurorehabilitation support
- Epilepsy management with continuous monitoring

### Patient Outcomes

- 85% of ischemic stroke patients achieve favorable outcomes
- Seizure control achieved in 80%+ of epilepsy patients
- Improved functional recovery through comprehensive rehabilitation
