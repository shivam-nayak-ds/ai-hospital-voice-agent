# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Pregnancy Guidelines

## Overview

Pregnancy is a beautiful journey lasting about 9 months or 40 weeks. Regular checkups and healthy habits ensure a safe pregnancy and a healthy baby. The Obstetrics Department at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital provides complete care from conception to delivery and postpartum recovery.

## Risk Factors for Complications

- Age below 18 or above 35 years
- Previous complicated pregnancies
- High blood pressure or diabetes before pregnancy
- Multiple pregnancies (twins or more)
- History of miscarriage or preterm delivery
- Being underweight or overweight
- Smoking, alcohol, or drug use
- Thyroid disorders or kidney disease

## Common Symptoms During Pregnancy

- Morning sickness especially in the first 3 months
- Fatigue and tiredness
- Frequent urination
- Back pain and body aches
- Swelling in feet and ankles
- Heartburn and indigestion
- Mood changes and emotional sensitivity
- Food cravings or aversions

## Important Prenatal Visits

- **First Visit**: As soon as pregnancy is confirmed
- **First Trimester**: Monthly visits with blood tests and ultrasound
- **Second Trimester**: Monthly visits with anomaly scan at 18 to 22 weeks
- **Third Trimester**: Every 2 weeks until 36 weeks, then weekly until delivery
- Additional visits if any complications arise

## Lifestyle Recommendations

Walk for 20 to 30 minutes daily unless your doctor advises otherwise. Sleep on your left side especially in later months. Avoid heavy lifting and strenuous exercise. Wear comfortable shoes and loose clothing. Stay away from smoking areas and strong chemicals. Manage stress with gentle yoga and deep breathing. Attend all scheduled prenatal appointments.

## Diet Guidelines

**Eat More**: Green leafy vegetables, fresh fruits, whole grains, dal, milk, curd, eggs, fish (well-cooked), nuts, and iron-rich foods.

**Eat Less**: Spicy food, fried food, caffeine (limit to 1 cup per day), processed food, and sugary snacks.

**Avoid Completely**: Raw or undercooked meat and eggs, unpasteurized milk, alcohol, smoking, and tobacco.

**Supplements**: Take iron, folic acid, and calcium tablets as prescribed by your doctor throughout pregnancy.

## Medication Guidance

- Never take any medicine without asking your doctor
- Continue prescribed supplements regularly
- Inform every doctor you visit that you are pregnant
- Avoid over-the-counter painkillers unless prescribed
- Report any medicine side effects to your doctor immediately
- Keep your vaccination record updated (tetanus, flu as recommended)

## When to Seek Medical Attention

- Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy
- Severe headache with vision changes
- Sudden swelling of face, hands, or feet
- Decreased baby movement after 28 weeks
- Pain or burning during urination
- Fever above 100.4°F
- Leaking of fluid from vagina
- Contractions before 37 weeks

## Emergency Warning Signs

Visit the emergency department at Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital immediately if you experience:

- Heavy vaginal bleeding with pain
- Severe abdominal pain
- Fits or seizures
- Severe breathlessness
- High fever not responding to medicine
- Baby stops moving completely
- Sudden severe headache with blurred vision

These may indicate serious conditions like preeclampsia, placental abruption, or preterm labor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I visit the doctor during pregnancy?**
A: At least 8 to 10 visits throughout pregnancy for normal cases.

**Q: Can I travel during pregnancy?**
A: Short travel is generally safe. Avoid long journeys especially in the last trimester. Ask your doctor first.

**Q: Is normal delivery possible after a previous cesarean?**
A: Yes, in many cases. Your doctor will assess and advise based on your specific situation.

**Q: When should I go to the hospital for delivery?**
A: Go when contractions are regular every 5 minutes, if water breaks, or if there is bleeding.
