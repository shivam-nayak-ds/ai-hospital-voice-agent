# Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital - Surgery Preparation Guidelines

## Overview

Surgery is a medical procedure where a doctor operates on your body to treat a disease or injury. Preparing well before surgery helps you recover faster and reduces complications. At Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital, our team ensures you are fully prepared physically and mentally before any surgical procedure.

## Before Surgery Checklist

- Visit your surgeon for a pre-operative consultation
- Complete all prescribed blood tests and investigations
- Inform your doctor about all medicines you take
- Tell your doctor about any allergies
- Arrange a family member or friend to accompany you
- Plan for leave from work during recovery
- Arrange home support for post-surgery care
- Complete insurance pre-authorization if applicable

## Medication Instructions

- Blood thinners like aspirin or warfarin may need to be stopped 5 to 7 days before surgery. Your doctor will guide you.
- Diabetes medicines may need adjustment. Follow your doctor's specific instructions.
- Blood pressure medicines are usually continued. Confirm with your surgeon.
- Herbal supplements and vitamins should be stopped one week before surgery.
- Bring all your current medicines to the hospital on admission day.

## Food and Drink Instructions

- Do not eat any food for 8 to 12 hours before surgery
- Do not drink water or any liquids for 4 to 6 hours before surgery
- Follow exact fasting instructions given by your surgical team
- Eating or drinking before surgery can cause dangerous complications during anesthesia
- Chewing gum and smoking are also not allowed during fasting

## What to Bring to Hospital

- Aadhar card or government photo identification
- Insurance card and pre-authorization documents
- All current medical reports and prescriptions
- Comfortable loose-fitting clothes for discharge day
- Personal hygiene items (toothbrush, soap, towel)
- Slippers with non-slip soles
- Mobile phone and charger
- List of emergency contact numbers

## Night Before Surgery

- Take a bath with antiseptic soap if instructed
- Wear clean and comfortable clothes
- Remove nail polish, jewelry, and contact lenses
- Get a good night's sleep
- Follow any bowel preparation instructions if given
- Confirm your arrival time with the hospital

## Day of Surgery

- Arrive at the hospital at the scheduled time
- Wear the hospital gown provided
- Remove dentures, hearing aids, and glasses before going to operation theater
- Your family can wait in the designated waiting area
- The surgical team will verify your identity and procedure before starting

## Post-Surgery Recovery

- You will be moved to the recovery room after surgery
- Nurses will monitor your vital signs continuously
- Pain medicines will be given as needed
- Eating and drinking will start as your doctor allows
- Walking and movement will begin early to prevent complications
- Follow all instructions from your surgical team

## When to Seek Medical Attention After Surgery

Contact Lifeline Multi-Speciality Hospital immediately if you experience:

- Fever above 101°F after surgery
- Increasing redness, swelling, or discharge from the wound
- Severe pain not relieved by prescribed medicines
- Difficulty breathing or chest pain
- Excessive bleeding from the surgical site
- Nausea and vomiting that does not stop
- Inability to pass urine for more than 8 hours

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How early should I reach the hospital?**
A: Arrive at least 2 hours before your scheduled surgery time.

**Q: Can I brush my teeth on surgery day?**
A: Yes, but do not swallow water. Spit out completely.

**Q: When can I eat after surgery?**
A: Your doctor will advise when to start eating. It depends on the type of surgery.

**Q: How long will I stay in the hospital?**
A: Stay duration varies from same day to several days depending on the procedure.

**Q: Can my family stay with me?**
A: One attendant is allowed to stay. ICU patients have limited visiting hours.
